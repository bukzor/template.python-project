# Why "buck is optional" is refuted

"Native substrate first, buck optional later" presupposes a native
cross-ecosystem substrate buck could optionally accelerate. No such substrate
exists — native workspaces are single-ecosystem islands
(claims.kb/native-workspaces-single-ecosystem.md). So the only way to obtain
cross-ecosystem composition + self-similarity at all is buck itself
(claims.kb/buck-is-unifying-spine.md). "Optional accelerator" therefore
mischaracterizes a load-bearing component; the claim is contradicted and
retracted.
